Recently, the writing process of the blog has been changed to **Chinese as the main language** and English is generated on demand, so I need to write an article to verify:

1. 只在 `app/(post)/zh` 目录新增稿件，首页是否立即展示？
2. `pnpm generate:english --post 2024/workflow-test` 没有执行的情况下，页面能否回退到中文标题？
3. `getPosts` 读取中文目录后，分页/排序有没有异常？

## Test process

- 建立 `app/(post)/zh/2024/workflow-test/page.mdx` 文件，并写入 `export const metadata`，重点放上 `publishedAt`（2024 年 11 月 30 日）。
- After starting the development server and refreshing the homepage, you can see that the new article is at the top of the 2024 list and the "Sort by Date" button is in effect.
- When switching to the English interface, the list can still display Chinese titles, indicating that the fallback logic is effective.
- 访问新文章详情页，`Header` 组件仍能根据语言切换标题和计数，说明我们传入的 `zh_title` 字段被正确解析。

## in conclusion

中文目录现在已经是**权威数据源**，只要写好 metadata，就能让文章进入首页和 API 输出。英文稿后续随时生成即可，不会阻塞发布。接下来考虑给脚本再加一个 `pnpm new:post --slug ...` 的脚手架，让新文章创建步骤更轻量。
